# Board Brief — RentIt (working name)

## The business

A hyperlocal peer-to-peer rental marketplace. People list idle items they own — tools, equipment, hobby gear, household items — and nearby users rent them on a per-day basis. The platform earns a commission (estimated 15–20%) on each completed rental transaction.

## Two-sided marketplace

**Owners (supply side):** People who own items sitting unused — a drill, a glue gun, a camping tent, a pressure washer. They list the item with photos, a daily price, and availability. They earn passive income from things they'd otherwise leave in a drawer or garage.

**Renters (demand side):** People who need an item for a short period and don't want to buy it. They discover nearby listings, request to rent, and pay per day.

## Transaction flow

1. Owner lists item (photos, price/day, location radius)
2. Renter nearby discovers item, sends rental request
3. Owner accepts
4. Payment is held in escrow
5. Item exchanged (in person, locally)
6. Item returned → platform releases funds to owner minus commission

## Revenue model

Commission on each transaction — estimated 15–20% of rental value. No subscription, no listing fee (at launch).

## Stage

Early — ideation and validation only. No product built, no team, no budget disclosed, no city chosen yet.

## What is not yet known

- Target city / geography for launch
- Founder's background and unfair advantages
- How the first supply (listings) and demand (renters) will be acquired
- How damage, disputes, and no-shows will be handled
- Whether any deposit or insurance mechanism will be used
- Exact commission structure and what the market will bear
