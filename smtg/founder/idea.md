# RentIt (working name)

- What it is: A hyperlocal peer-to-peer marketplace where people rent out idle items (tools, equipment, gear) to neighbours, and the platform earns a commission on each transaction.
- Who it is for: Two sides — (1) owners of idle household/hobby/trade items who want passive income from things sitting unused, and (2) renters who need an item short-term and don't want to buy it.
- What it sells, at what price: Access to a local rental marketplace. Platform charges a commission (estimated 15–20%) on each rental transaction, split between or charged to renter.
- Where and how: Hyperlocal — starts in one city. Renters discover items nearby via app/web. Owner lists item with photos, price/day, and availability. Renter requests → owner accepts → payment held in escrow → item exchanged → platform releases funds minus commission.
- Budget and constraints: Early stage, ideation/validation phase. No budget, tech, or team size specified yet.
